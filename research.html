Mechanism: sparse cover under a tolerant metric

Distance weighting

The official metric gives a prediction partial credit when it is within a 300 m triangular-kernel radius of a hidden fault. This can make sparse, near-line detections useful even when rasterization or mapping is not pixel-perfect.

Redundant mass

A thick line can spend predictions on locations already covered by a nearby maximum. Thinning and near-catalogue pruning may reduce waste, but they are emission changes—not proof that the surface corresponds to real faults.

Geological caution

Terrain ridges and thermal/mineral anomalies can be caused by lithology, erosion, drainage, land cover, or non-geothermal alteration. A competition score alone cannot establish a causal geothermal-vent mechanism.

Can this project beat the old value?

In principle, a better surface and emitter can score higher. This checkout cannot demonstrate that: it has no lane assignment, data, evaluator, registry, or candidate. The official public leaderboard showed 0.4413 at the top when read on 2026-10-21, but that is a public-page observation—not our score or a private result.
